## Suggested Angle

InsiderLLM should focus on *practical, real-world performance* for a given budget. Many articles focus on theoretical specs. We should emphasize:

*   **VRAM as the limiting factor:** Clearly explain how much VRAM is *actually* needed for different model sizes and quantization levels, referencing our VRAM guide.
*   **Bandwidth’s impact:** Highlight the importance of memory bandwidth, especially for CPU-offloading scenarios and the cost of offloading layers.
*   **AMD ROCm caveats:** Be honest about the challenges of ROCm compatibility and provide clear guidance for users who want to use AMD GPUs.
*   **Used market value:** Focus on the best *value* in the used market, not just the fastest cards. The RTX 3060 12GB is likely the sweet spot for budget builds.
*   **Realistic expectations:** Don’t overhype any card. Be clear about the trade-offs between price, performance, and VRAM capacity.
