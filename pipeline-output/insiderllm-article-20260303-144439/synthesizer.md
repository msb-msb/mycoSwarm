## Suggested Angle

InsiderLLM should focus on *realistic* local AI expectations for budget GPUs. Many sites hype up the potential of running large models, but fail to address the performance hit of offloading layers to system RAM. Our coverage should emphasize:

*   **Honest performance expectations:** Show real-world token/s numbers for common models and quantization levels, and clearly explain the impact of VRAM limitations.
*   **Total cost of ownership:** Factor in the cost of RAM upgrades (DDR4/DDR5) needed for offloading, and highlight the benefit of maximizing VRAM to minimize reliance on slower system RAM.
*   **Practical advice:** Guide readers through the process of choosing the right GPU *for their specific use case* and budget, considering model size, quantization, and RAM availability.
*   **Software compatibility:** Be upfront about the challenges of ROCm on AMD cards and provide clear guidance on verifying compatibility.
*   **Focus on value:** Highlight GPUs that offer the best balance of performance, VRAM, and price, even if they aren't the newest or most powerful options.
